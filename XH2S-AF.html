Step 01 // Drive Behavior

Focus Modes (AF-S, AF-C, MF)

Menu: AF/MF SETTING > FOCUS MODE
AF-S SINGLE AUTOFOCUS

Half-pressing the shutter locks focus once. The AF box turns green upon lock. If you or the subject move afterward, the camera will not refocus until you re-press.

Best For: Landscapes, static portraits, architecture, macro, and low-contrast stationary scenes.
ACTION CORE
AF-C CONTINUOUS AF

Continuously recalculates focus distance and predicts subject trajectory while holding half-shutter or AF-ON, even during 15–40 fps bursts.

Best For: Sports, wildlife, birds in flight, vehicles, and active subjects moving toward/away from the lens.
MF MANUAL FOCUS

Direct lens ring focus enhanced by Focus Peak Highlight, Digital Split Image, and Focus Check auto-zoom. Press AF-ON for Instant AF override.

Best For: Astrophotography, shooting through fences/glass, and critical product/studio focus.

Movie Mode // AF/MF Setting

How X-H2S Video Autofocus Operates

6.2K / 4K 120P CONTINUOUS AF

When the Mode Dial is turned to the Movie Icon, the X-H2S switches to a streamlined cinema-oriented AF menu. Unlike stills photography—which offers Zone and generic Wide/Tracking—video autofocus relies on two core area modes combined with Face/Eye or Subject Detection, plus customizable focus transition speeds.

MOVIE AF MODE: MULTI AUTOMATIC

The camera automatically evaluates the entire frame and selects the most prominent subject (prioritizing detected Faces/Eyes or Subject Detection targets).

USE CASE: Run-and-gun vlogging, gimbal tracking shots, or solo interviews where the subject moves freely across the frame.
MOVIE AF MODE: AREA TARGETED BOX

Focuses strictly on whatever enters your positioned focus box. Note: In Movie mode, the AF box size is fixed, and there is no generic "tap-to-track" object lock unless using Face/Eye or Subject Detection.

USE CASE: Controlled product demonstrations, tripod talking-heads, and intentional touchscreen rack-focus pulls.

DURING RECORDING

Refocusing Live

While recording is active, you can force an AF recalculation or shift focus points at any time by half-pressing the shutter button, pressing AF-ON, or tapping the rear LCD touchscreen.

MANUAL ASSIST

MF Focus Meter & Check Lock

In Movie Manual Focus, the X-H2S adds a visual Focus Meter scale alongside Focus Peaking. Enable FOCUS CHECK LOCK if you want punched-in magnification to stay active after hitting Record.

OPTICS PAIRING

Linear Motor (LM) Lenses

Video AF speed and silence depend heavily on lens actuators. Fujinon LM (Linear Motor) lenses (e.g., XF18-120mm F4 PZ, XF16-55mm, XF33mm F1.4) deliver silent, step-free cinema focus pulls.
