Workflow Tip 01 • Custom Functions

Map Film Sim to an Fn Button

The X-H2S features 14 customizable physical buttons and 4 touchscreen swipe directions. Assign FILM SIMULATION to one of the top-plate Fn buttons next to the OLED sub-monitor so you can switch emulsions without taking your eye off the EVF.

Workflow Tip 02 • Film Sim Bracketing

3 Looks From 1 Shutter Press

Unsure whether a fast-moving scene calls for punchy color or gritty black-and-white? Set the Drive mode to FILM SIMULATION BKT to simultaneously record three distinct simulations (e.g., NOSTALGIC Neg., CLASSIC CHROME, and ACROS+R) from a single exposure.

Workflow Tip 03 • 10-Bit HEIF & Video

10-Bit Stills & ETERNA Video

Pair your favorite Film Simulations with 10-bit 4:2:2 HEIF stills for smoother tonal gradations at 30% smaller file sizes than JPEG. In Movie mode, ETERNA and ETERNA BLEACH BYPASS provide cinema-ready dynamic range straight out of camera without needing Log grading.
